## Scope discipline

No major architectural rewrite was required for the confirmed clock defect. A raw composition time and per-recording modulo preserve the existing single AudioContext, crossfades, explicit consent and route lifecycle. Tests retain the two recording durations independently. The correction changes playback bookkeeping only; it does not resample recordings or alter source data/assets.

Performance work would cross provider, contract and lifecycle boundaries, so it is documented as a measured profiling task rather than an unverified “optimization.” New species audio, a more cinematic renderer and broad spacing changes require asset or design review; they were not improvised during this final audit.
