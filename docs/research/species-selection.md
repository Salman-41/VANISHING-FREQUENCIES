# Species selection

**Decision date / last verification:** 2026-10-08. Select six primary species: snow leopard, blue whale, tiger, Bornean orangutan, hawksbill sea turtle and African forest elephant. Red panda and Asian elephant remain reserves.

## Selection approach

Prioritize traceable survey methods and dated evidence, contrasting habitats, identifiable intervention stories, distinctive visual material, and compatible item-level media licenses. The judgments below are editorial selection judgments, not biological scores. Selection does not imply a complete global population series or final asset clearance.

## Boundaries of the choice

“Orangutan” denotes multiple species. This foundation uses Bornean orangutan only; do not attach Sumatran or Tapanuli estimates or recordings to its record. Forest elephant likewise excludes savanna elephant and combined African elephant totals. The blue whale measurement is a named stock of the northern subspecies, not the whole species.

The six selections balance habitats and intervention evidence. They are not a representative sample of world biodiversity: five are mammals, one a reptile, and Africa/Asia and marine environments dominate. Do not aggregate their estimates into a biodiversity index or a total lost-animal statistic.

## Acquisition priorities

1. Confirm permitted Red List use and obtain formal assessment dates, IDs/versions and latest-assessment metadata for all selected species. Retain unknown values until access and metadata are verified.
2. Retrieve original Nepal census reports and the Bornean estimate's original model; review demographic units, survey windows, uncertainty and comparability. The inaccessible [Nepal report lead](https://dnpwc.gov.np/media/files/Status_of_Tigers_Ic2ylSC.pdf) is not evidence already acquired.
3. Inspect the newer [forest elephant status report](https://iucn.org/resources/publication/african-forest-elephant-loxodonta-cyclotis-status-report-2024) and its rights. Its full text was not verified in this pass.
4. Acquire compatible media only after preserving item-page license, creator, exact credit, original source, context, retrieval date and changes. Obtain wild site-specific images for intervention chapters; the three captive portraits cannot depict wild recovery sites.
5. Source snow leopard, tiger and Bornean orangutan audio with verified identity and rights. Obtain Cornell permission if its forest elephant example is selected. Confirm the blue whale clip's original provenance and playback speed. Hawksbill ambience must be described as habitat sound.

The foundation is ready for development with explicit unknown fields, held statistics and unavailable-media states. Evidence gaps are not authorization to manufacture a trend or substitute a different species.
