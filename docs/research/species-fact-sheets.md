## Reading and display rules

- `dated-context-only` permits a historical estimate only with its full geography, population unit, data period, source edition and uncertainty. It does not mean current or global.
- `hold` means verified as a public announcement or historical report, with insufficient underlying methods/period evidence for a displayed statistic. These entries are research notes only.
- Assessment date/year is unknown for every record. Public conservation categories were checked; formal assessment metadata and latest-assessment confirmation were not acquired under approved Red List access. A news date is not an assessment date.
- No audio/image has been acquired or cleared for project use. Candidate licenses are item-page findings; acquisition must confirm source provenance and credits.
- Editorial headings and premises are interpretations, not scientific findings. The tagline is editorial; this dataset does not establish a measured worldwide decline in sound.
